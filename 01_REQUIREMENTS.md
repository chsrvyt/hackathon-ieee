# AttendAI — Requirements

## Product Goal

Build a deployable Attendance Shortage Early Warning & Condonation System.

The system must predict attendance shortage early rather than merely displaying current attendance.

## Roles

### Student
- View own overall attendance
- View subject attendance
- View projected attendance
- View risk level and reason
- View recovery recommendation
- View alerts
- Submit condonation request
- View condonation status

### Mentor
- View assigned students
- Identify at-risk students
- View trends
- Review condonation requests

### HOD/Admin
- Department analytics
- High-risk students
- Shortage reports
- Review workflow

### Exam Cell
- Shortage/eligibility reporting
- Condonation decision visibility

## P0

- Login
- Role authorization
- CSV/XLSX import
- Attendance calculation
- Projection
- Risk classification
- Student dashboard
- Mentor dashboard
- Admin/HOD dashboard

## P1

- Recovery calculator
- Alerts
- Condonation workflow
- Department reports

## P2

- Parent notifications
- Medical proof upload
- Advanced ML
- External notifications
- Advanced live analytics

## Definition of Done

A judge can log in, upload attendance, inspect processed data, select a student, understand current/projected attendance, understand risk, see recovery action, review mentor-side risk, use condonation workflow, and inspect department reporting.

## Product principle

Don't just show attendance. Predict the problem early and recommend the next action.
