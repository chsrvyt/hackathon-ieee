# AttendAI — Judge Demo Script

## Demo story

Show one student's journey from normal attendance monitoring to early intervention.

## Step 1 — Admin

Login as Admin.

Say:

> "The system accepts institutional attendance data and converts it into actionable risk information."

Upload the sample attendance file.

Show:
- successful validation
- processed records
- any validation summary

## Step 2 — Analytics

Open the dashboard.

Show:
- total students
- safe/warning/critical counts
- shortage list

Select a high-risk student.

## Step 3 — Explainability

Show:

```text
Current attendance
Target attendance
Recent trend
Projected attendance
Risk level
Reason
```

Say:

> "The important part is that the system doesn't just show red. It explains why the student is at risk."

## Step 4 — Recovery

Open recovery calculator.

Show how many future classes must be attended to reach the target, or explain if recovery is impossible under the current assumptions.

## Step 5 — Mentor

Login as Mentor.

Show:
- assigned students
- high-risk students
- alerts
- trend

## Step 6 — Student

Login as Student.

Show:
- personal attendance
- risk
- recommended action
- alerts

Submit a condonation request.

## Step 7 — Review

Login as Mentor/Admin.

Open pending condonation request.

Approve/reject it.

Return to Student.

Show updated status.

## Step 8 — Department

Open department report.

Show:
- total students
- shortage count
- risk distribution
- subject/department pattern

## Closing statement

> "AttendAI turns attendance from a passive percentage into an early-warning and intervention workflow."
